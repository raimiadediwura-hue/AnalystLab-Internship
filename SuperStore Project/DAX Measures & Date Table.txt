DAX Measures & Date Table

Date = 
    ADDCOLUMNS(CALENDARAUTO(),
        "Year",YEAR([Date]),
        "Quarter", "Q" & QUARTER([Date]),
        "Month No", MONTH([Date]),
        "Month Name", FORMAT([Date], "MMM"),
        "Day", DAY([Date]))


Measures 

1. Total Sales = SUM('Superstore dataset'[Sales])
2. Total Profit = SUM('Superstore dataset'[Profit])
3. Total Orders = DISTINCTCOUNT('Superstore dataset'[Order ID])
4. Profit Margin = DIVIDE([Total Profit], [Total Sales], 0)
5. Average Sales = AVERAGE('Superstore dataset'[Sales])
6. Quantity Sold = SUM('Superstore dataset'[Quantity])
